Reel 6 · TC 01:50:00:00The open question

Where accountability goes missing

A feature is one click inside a tool the artist already trusts. Follow the shot downstream and see who can know.

Step 1

Software vendor

Knows what the model is and what trained it.
Step 2

Editor or artist

Knows a button was pressed. May not know what sits behind it.
Step 3

Post house

Sees the delivered shot. The method may not be logged.
Step 4

Studio or distributor

Often relies on a signed deliverables sheet.
Step 5

Audience

Told nothing on screen, in most cases.
Scenario A

The language swap

One click dubs and subtitles a whole feature. Dialogue is replaced, the actor's voice may be cloned, the lips may be redrawn. Did the actor consent? Was the translation checked by a person?

Scenario B

The two second plate

A stable establishing shot of New York in 1966, requested and delivered in minutes. No archive licence, no photographer, no credit. Is it presented as real? Who decided?

Four questions every tool should be able to answer

What changedDid it generate, alter or replace picture or sound?
Whose workWhat data trained the model, and was it licensed?
Whose face and voiceWas a performer's likeness copied or synthesized?
Who approvedWho signed off, and where is that recorded?
