    What the stack shows

    The lower layers follow rules a person wrote, so every step can be checked. The upper layers make probable guesses and are built from data nobody on the project saw. The higher the layer, the harder it is to say who decided what.

    The oldest layer can also help. Rule based checks are already how deliveries are policed. Adobe stamps Generative Extend results with Content Credentials, and Netflix asks production partners to tell it before they use generative AI. Put those two ideas together and disclosure could be checked automatically before a show ships.
